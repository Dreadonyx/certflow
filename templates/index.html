Step 6 — Throttled Mailer Gmail-safe queue


            

                Sends one email at a time, spaced 3–5 min apart with ±30 % jitter and a 15–25 min
                break every 15–20 emails. State is persisted in the app's SQLite database so restarts never
                re-send already-delivered certificates. Wire up a free
                cron-job.org
                job pointing at /mailer/send-next?secret=MAILER_SECRET every 4 minutes to
                drive delivery automatically.
